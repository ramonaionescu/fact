Browser storage is not a backup. Export your project regularly.

Your browser is your workspace.

This app stores the current project on this browser and device. It has no server database, login, analytics, or background upload of your scenario content.

Export project creates a .json backup with every saved scenario, all used design versions, your current setup, and your unfinished draft. Use Import project to restore it or move it to another computer. Import replaces the open project; it does not merge projects.

Export all CSV is for spreadsheets and analysis. Export all text is for reading or copying the scenarios. These exports contain saved scenarios, not an unfinished draft.

Clearing site data, changing browsers, using private browsing, or changing the website address can remove or separate your workspace. Files opened directly from your computer may not retain storage consistently. Keep a JSON backup and use the hosted site for regular work.

People who visit the same hosted app have separate browser workspaces. There is no shared team database. Browser storage is not encrypted by this app; do not use it for confidential participant records.

The possible-combination count is the product of the numbers of levels. It does not test plausibility, impose constraints, randomize assignments, or guarantee a balanced experimental sample.
